About me

I find out why a process is slow or a number is wrong, build the fix, and make sure people can use it.

I studied finance at North South University in Dhaka, then moved to Brisbane for a Master of Business in Business Analytics at Griffith University, graduating in 2025 with Distinction. That pairing shapes how I work: I read a ledger like an accountant and question it like an analyst.

At Quinlan Consulting in Melbourne, I diagnosed a month-end general ledger bottleneck generating more than eight hours of manual rework per cycle and developed an Excel automation solution that reduced processing time by 50%+. During the same internship, I resolved a $50,000 AUD budget discrepancy through cross-source investigation and implemented verification controls.

Earlier, I co-founded a startup in Dhaka that secured places in four competitive incubation programmes. Since July 2024 I have also worked part-time in customer service at Viva Retail Energy, managing 50+ customer interactions a day and turning recurring complaint patterns into data-driven feedback.

  1. Step 01Diagnose

    Find the real bottleneck or the source of the error before proposing anything.

  2. Step 02Build

    Automate the work, with validation logic built into the solution.

  3. Step 03Embed

    Document it, train people and add controls so the fix lasts.
